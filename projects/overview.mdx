Every project I build starts with a question: *what would make this easier, faster, or just more enjoyable to work with?* Whether it's automating a tedious workflow, shipping a reusable component library, or exploring a new technology through a weekend experiment, I approach each project with a bias toward simplicity and real-world utility. Browse the categories below to find work that resonates with what you're looking for.

    - **Node.js** — Powers the majority of my back-end services and CLI tools

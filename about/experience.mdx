My career has been a continuous thread of curiosity, ownership, and growth — from shipping my first production feature as a junior developer to leading cross-functional engineering initiatives at scale. Each role has shaped how I think about software: not just as code to be written, but as a product to be understood, a system to be maintained, and a craft to be refined. Below is a reverse-chronological look at the positions that have defined that journey.

## Work History

<Steps>
  <Step title="Senior Software Engineer at TechCorp — 2022–Present">
    TechCorp is a B2B SaaS platform serving over 2 million users across the logistics and supply-chain space. As a senior engineer on the Platform team, I drive the design and delivery of high-impact, customer-facing features.

    - Architected and shipped a real-time shipment tracking dashboard using React, WebSockets, and a Go-based data-streaming service, reducing average page load time by 42 %.
    - Led the migration of a monolithic Node.js API to domain-aligned microservices, improving deployment independence and cutting service-level incident recovery time in half.
    - Established a team-wide TypeScript strict-mode migration plan, eliminating an entire class of runtime type errors across 120 000 lines of existing code.
    - Mentored three mid-level engineers through bi-weekly 1:1s and structured code-review feedback, two of whom were promoted within 18 months.

    <Tip>
      **Key achievement:** The real-time tracking dashboard was adopted by TechCorp's three largest enterprise accounts within 60 days of launch, directly contributing to a contract renewal worth $1.8 M ARR.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Software Engineer at StartupXYZ — 2020–2022">
    StartupXYZ was an early-stage fintech startup building an automated expense-management product for SMBs. I joined as the fourth engineering hire and helped grow the team to fifteen engineers while wearing many hats along the way.

    - Built the core transaction-categorisation engine in Python (FastAPI + PostgreSQL), processing over 500 000 transactions per day with 99.97 % uptime.
    - Owned the CI/CD pipeline end-to-end using GitHub Actions and Docker, reducing average deployment time from 45 minutes to under 8 minutes.
    - Collaborated closely with the design team in Figma to implement a component library in React that unified the visual language across web and mobile web surfaces.
    - Introduced automated integration and end-to-end testing with Playwright, increasing overall test coverage from 18 % to 74 % in six months.

    <Tip>
      **Key achievement:** The redesigned onboarding flow — which I prototyped and shipped solo — increased trial-to-paid conversion by 31 %, a result that was cited in the company's Series A pitch deck.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Junior Developer at AgencyABC — 2018–2020">
    AgencyABC is a full-service digital agency delivering websites, e-commerce stores, and web applications for clients across retail, hospitality, and media. This role gave me a rapid-fire education in shipping real software under real deadlines.

    - Developed and maintained more than a dozen client sites using JavaScript, React, and various headless CMS platforms, consistently delivering on time and within scope.
    - Collaborated with designers and project managers in fast-paced, two-week sprint cycles, building strong habits around estimation, communication, and scope management.
    - Optimised front-end performance for a high-traffic retail client, improving Lighthouse scores from the mid-50s to consistently above 90 and reducing bounce rate by 19 %.
    - Took ownership of the agency's internal component library, documenting each component and introducing Storybook to streamline design-to-development handoff.

    <Tip>
      **Key achievement:** Independently delivered a full e-commerce site rebuild for a national retail brand in five weeks — two weeks ahead of the original schedule — earning recognition in the agency's quarterly all-hands.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Freelance Web Developer — 2016–2018">
    Before entering full-time employment, I built a small freelance practice developing websites and lightweight web apps for local businesses, non-profits, and independent creators. This early experience taught me the fundamentals of client management, scoping, and self-directed learning.

    - Delivered over 20 projects ranging from portfolio sites to custom booking systems, managing requirements, timelines, and client communication independently.
    - Gained hands-on exposure to JavaScript, PHP, MySQL, and early React while self-teaching through documentation, open-source code, and community forums.
    - Built a reputation for clear communication and reliable delivery that generated repeat business and referrals — the foundation that eventually led to my first full-time offer.

    <Info>
      This period of freelancing gave me an appreciation for the business context behind every technical decision — a perspective I still carry into team settings today.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>
